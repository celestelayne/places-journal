# NNNN. Short title in title case

**Status:** Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](./NNNN-slug.md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

Two to four sentences. What forced this decision? What is the problem or
constraint that made a choice necessary?

## Considered options

- **Option A — name.** One or two sentences describing it.
- **Option B — name.** One or two sentences.
- **Option C — name.** One or two sentences.

## Decision

One to three sentences. Which option was chosen. Written as a decision, not a
suggestion: "We use X." not "We should probably use X."

## Consequences

**Positive**
- What this makes easier or safer.

**Negative and trade-offs**
- What this makes harder or forecloses. Every decision has costs; name them.

**Follow-up**
- Anything this decision leaves unresolved for a future ADR.
